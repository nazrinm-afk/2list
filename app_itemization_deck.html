SLIDE 1 OF 10 · MASTER INDEX

2list TV Magazine System Architecture

Use this deck as our shared vocabulary. Every visual element has a distinct code (e.g. IT-Q2) so we never confuse different boxes or cards.

PWA Standalone & Web
⊞ 1. The 5 Navigation Tabs
  • TAB-1
    Home (Overview) Series progress, stats, and vertical episode stack.
  • TAB-2
    Episode (Lineup) Hero card, location, special theme, gear edit, capsules.
  • TAB-3
    Item (Slider / Split) Questions card vs Capsule card, and peeking tabs.
  • TAB-4
    Continuity (Conti Sheet) Camera / audio takes, good take (GT), host throws.
  • TAB-5
    Script (Editor & Rundown) Video / Audio split columns and rundown timing.
☰ 2. The Item Tab Dual System
BOX-Q Questions Box Interview Stack

Holds clickable cards for interview capsules. Contains:
• Active upcoming questions (sorted by date)
• Filmed questions section at bottom
• Opens full-screen On-Set Interview sheet modal

BOX-C Capsule Box Production Log

Holds all registered stories in the project. Contains:
• "＋ Add Capsule" button & search toolbar
• Active capsule rows (IV/OT badges, keys, topics)
• Cancelled section (stripped from episode/capsule)

❐ 3. Popups & Dialogs
  • DLG-1 Episode Settings Dialog

    Triggered by gear icon on Episode Hero. Edits Location, Special Theme, and Sponsored checkbox.

  • DLG-2 Capsule Record Dialog

    Full capsule editor: Topic, Type (IV/OT), Guest, Location, Date, Status, Assignment, and Questions.

  • DLG-3 Interview Questions Sheet Modal

    Spacious on-set sheet displaying numbered questions for shooting interviews.

H-BOX1 Episode Pill & Sponsored Indicator

Each episode box on the Home tab starts with the `EPISODE NN` badge. If marked as sponsored, the yellow `★ SPONSORED` badge is displayed right beside it.

H-BOX2 Location Row (With Yellow Map Pin)

Located on the line immediately below the episode number. Features a prominent yellow map pin icon with white location title text (e.g. Langkawi, Penang).

H-BOX3 Special Theme Subtitle

Positioned directly under the location. Custom tagline or theme for this specific episode (e.g. "Empowering Women & Wellness").

H-BOX4 Capsule Count Progress

Reports how many stories have been assigned to this episode out of the series target (e.g. "6 of 6 capsules recorded for this episode").

IT-SPLIT-Q Card 1: "Questions" Cover Card

The left card in the Item tab. Displays the title "Questions".
• When you tap this card, it expands into the full Questions Box and shrinks the Capsule card into a yellow peeking tab on the right.

IT-SPLIT-C Card 2: "Capsule" Cover Card

The right card in the Item tab. Displays the title "Capsule".
• When you tap this card, it expands into the full Capsule Story Records Log and shrinks the Questions card into a yellow peeking tab on the left.

Gesture & Navigation: You can tap either card to open it, or swipe left/right to glide between Questions and Capsule.

IT-Q1 Kotak Questions Utama (Expanded Panel)

The large main dark panel containing interview question sets for upcoming filming sessions.

IT-Q2 Kotak Setiap Set Soalan (Clickable Question Row Cards)

The individual cards inside the questions box:
• EP pill & Capsule pill: Tells you episode and capsule number.
• Topic & Guest: Topic title, interviewee name, shoot date.
• Arrow ↗: Tapping any card opens the spacious On-Set Interview sheet popup.

IT-Q3 Seksyen FILMED (Completed Questions)

Positioned at the bottom of the question list with a dashed divider. Question sets that have passed their date or are marked FILMED drop here automatically to keep active questions clean.

IT-Q4 Tab Kuning Kapsul di Sebelah Kanan (Right Peeking Tab)

The 42px yellow vertical bar on the right edge with vertical text "Capsule ›". Tapping it smoothly transitions to the Capsule records view.

IT-C1 Tab Kuning Questions di Sebelah Kiri (Left Peeking Tab)

The 42px yellow vertical bar on the left edge with text "‹ Questions". Tapping it smoothly returns to the Questions panel.

IT-C2 Kotak Capsule Records Utama (Expanded Panel)

The large main panel holding all production story records, filter tools, and the Add Capsule button.

IT-C3 Kotak Record Capsule (Active Story Cards)

Cards showing capsule assignment (`EP 01 / 1`), circular badge (`IV` for Interview or `OT` for Other), topic, location, shoot date, and status pill.

IT-C4 Seksyen CANCELLED (Muted Cancelled Stories)

Positioned at the bottom of the capsule list with a dashed red border. When a capsule is cancelled, its episode and capsule number are stripped and it is dropped here while remaining fully clickable.

CT-1 Scene / Host Throw Tabs

Horizontal tab row allowing on-set script supervisors to jump between Throw 1..N, Opening, Promo, and Closing.

CT-2 Take Logger & Auto File Numbering

Logs chronological file numbers across Camera 1, 2, 3 and external Audio recorder. Numbering never resets mid-day to prevent media overwrite.

CT-3 GT (Good Take) / NG Toggles & PDF Export

Marks the chosen take as Good Take (highlighted in emerald green for the editor). Tapping Share exports the official PDF log.

SLIDE 10 OF 10 · MODALS & POPUPS

Complete Dialog Catalog (Popups & Modals)

CATALOG: dialogs
DLG-1 Gear Button Trigger
Episode Settings Dialog

Opened by the gear icon on Episode Tab Hero.

Field 1: Location
Field 2: Special Theme
Field 3: Sponsored Checkbox
Function: `episodeOverviewDialog()`
DLG-2 Story Record Editor
Capsule Record Dialog

Opened by tapping any Capsule row or "＋ Add Capsule".

Fields: Topic, Guest, Title, Location
Type: Interview (IV) / Other (OT)
Assignment: Episode + Capsule #
Status: PREPRO, FILMED, EDITED, TX, CANCELLED
Function: `recordDialog()`
DLG-3 On-Set Interview Sheet
Interview Questions Popup

Opened by tapping any Question card on the Questions Box.

Header: Episode, Capsule, Topic, Guest
Body: Numbered list of primary questions & follow-ups in high-readability on-set format.
Function: `viewInterviewQuestionsPopup()`
